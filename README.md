# Hi, I'm Mahir 👋

I'm a CS student at **NJIT** (class of 2028) and a full-stack engineer who ships his own products. I grew up in Bangladesh and live in New Jersey.

Most of my time goes into AI-native apps: Next.js and FastAPI on the front and back, Postgres underneath, and LLMs doing the heavy lifting. Before that I was a full-stack intern at **Flowsurf**, where I moved a Postgres schema off JSONB and rebuilt the app's state layer with Zustand and React Query.

**Looking for:** Summer 2027 SWE and ML/data engineering internships. If you're hiring, [message me on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mahir-mohammed-labib-bb3085209/).
